geometry

shared-util

Conversions between maze-cell space and Roblox world space, plus the Chebyshev distance every streaming radius is measured in.

Cell space runs negative along world X — cell (1, 0) sits at a smaller X than cell (0, 0) — so these conversions are not plain scaling and the sign flip must not be dropped.

cframeToMazePosition​

</>
geometry.cframeToMazePosition(cframe: CFrame) → MazePosition

The cell a world-space CFrame falls in.

The inverse of [geometry.getCenterPosition]: cell x spans world X from -(x + 1) * MAZE_CELL_SIZE up to -x * MAZE_CELL_SIZE, which is where the walls either side of it are built. Subtracting half a cell before flooring shifts those bounds by half a cell, so everything in the near half of a cell reports as its neighbour and an observer's cell is off by one for half of every cell it crosses.

Chebyshev distance from a point to the nearest of several frames.

Chebyshev rather than Euclidean because streaming works in square rings: a radius of 2 should mean the 5x5 block of chunks around the observer, corners included.
